Check runtime/source/checksum ZIPs, CRC, extracted hashes, version/defaults, license/provenance, secret/private-path exclusion, no BGA weights and the existing eighteen NK3 models. Prior local evidence is not fresh release evidence. Record initial failures and resolutions in local release evidence. Physical input/IME/listening, OBS, long play, other PCs, arbitrary charts/skins/models, actual two-PC play and real-account production uploads remain unverified unless directly tested.
