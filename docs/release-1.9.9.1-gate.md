# TenRiff 1.9.9.1 verification gates

This is the release verification plan, not a completed-check or publication claim. Start from published 1.9.9 commit `79433ba2676237fdf0480363ebc1a4c81e9aa94e` plus the preserved 2026-10-24 ultrawide/LN candidate. The general fixed baseline remains 1.9.6.

The candidate's build, unit, native-session, visual-geometry and saved-replay evidence is prior local evidence. It does not certify new versioned binaries, packages, independent builds, remote CI or uploaded assets. Fresh release checks below are pending at document preparation; record final outcomes and failures in the release evidence before publication. The [release page](https://github.com/10kseason/TenRiff/releases/tag/1.9.9.1) determines public status.

- Build fresh Release targets; run focused ultrawide, LN visibility, mine-boundary and replay tests, then full CTest and native menu/session smoke. Check released player/ghost bodies, removal of judged heads and active glow, final-tail result waiting with negative visual offsets, and explicit skip/abort/game-over exceptions.
- Check 2560×1080, 3440×1440 and 5120×2160 geometry, full-width backgrounds/BGA/dimming, uniform centered UI, pointer mapping and explicit inset skin rectangles. Compare the inherited 16:9 behavior. Distinguish automated captures and handler dispatch from physical ultrawide-monitor/input testing.
- Verify same-lane mine clearance of at least 50ms at boundaries and multiple rates/seeds, other-lane independence, preserved source LN/keysounds/mines and no-mine v2/v3 parity. Round-trip saved v1/v2/v3 replay/results, preserve historical reconstruction, reject unknown versions, exclude mismatched ghosts, and use an on-disk BMS mine fixture with the native verifier. Check current v3 replay rejection by the old 1.9.9 verifier separately.
- Preserve timing, grades, R1–R10, peer v7, NK2 E and previous functionality. Keep non-v1 LN Mix outside legacy BEST/Sites and retain Guest/custom exclusions and `client_submitted`. No website, DB or public skin-editor deployment is required.
- Run the bundled offline skin-editor and catalog checks, scene-music, FFmpeg and Safetensors checks. The catalog update follows source references after the background helper change; preserve its editable IDs and values. Record any optional unavailable check without turning it into a pass.
- Require exact-head PR and merged-main CI. Build runtime/source/checksum assets in fresh directories; check ZIP CRC, hashes, version/default consistency, no private paths/credentials/user media, no BGA weights, and only the existing eighteen NK3 models. Construct final source from merged Git blobs, independently rebuild/test it, and compare preservation receipts for earlier releases, candidates and installations.
- Download and verify all three draft assets, then fresh anonymous public assets after publication. Preserve previous releases, tags and assets; never move an old tag or replace an earlier asset. Keep the original local candidate and its evidence unchanged.

Physical input/IME/listening, real ultrawide monitors, long play, other PCs, arbitrary charts/skins/models, actual two-PC play and real-account production uploads remain separate unverified outcomes unless new direct evidence is recorded. Initialization checks alone do not prove scored ghost playback. Record failed attempts and their resolution rather than silently discarding them.
