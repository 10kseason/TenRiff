# TenRiff 1.9.9.9-hotfix4

Safe and Fast (Minimal) use the available logical CPU threads for initial folder indexing, F5 rescans and existing-cache loading. ALL SONG also validates cached chart paths in parallel. Remaining charts and available RAM determine concurrency.

Successful judgement, score and hit-burst motion lasts 0.5 seconds by default. Tap and long-note heads disappear immediately on PG, GR or GOOD. BAD, POOR and missed notes continue below the judgement line.

Open Options → Skin → Background & Key Effects. Burst sizes are Normal 100%, Large 130% and Larger 165%. Choose Prism, Ring, Spark, Shockwave, Shards, Pixels or Image. Palettes are Soft Default, Pastel, Oil Paint, Monochrome (pale gray-white) and Cyberpunk.

Select a PNG or JPEG for image mode. It uses the source colors, aspect ratio and PNG transparency. The local path is saved in this device's profile. A missing image displays Prism. Under Judgement & HUD, choose judgement line Type 2 and select its default green outline or a solid fill color.

Existing R13/R14 replay and leaderboard identities apply. The fixed baseline is 1.9.6.
