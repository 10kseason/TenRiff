# Held adjustments and default speed keys

2026-10-27 · Local follow-up to 1.9.9.1; unpublished, no version bump. General fixed baseline remains 1.9.6.

- Fresh defaults are **F1 note speed −0.25 / F2 +0.25**. F3/F4 line up/down 1%, F5/F6 speed ×0.5/×2, and F7/F8 visual latency −/+1ms remain.
- Explicit saved bindings, including old defaults, stay unchanged. Existing profiles can assign the speed decrease/increase actions under Keymap > Common Keys or Profile.
- Hold Left/Right on the lobby quick-setting speed/latency rows, or hold −/+ for Rate. Gameplay, opening countdown and pause use the same acceleration for speed, line position and visual latency.
- The initial press changes one step. Existing first-repeat delays remain 250ms in the lobby and 180ms in gameplay. Repeat intervals are 45ms, then 25ms after 0.6s held, then 15ms after 1.5s. Step sizes and limits stay unchanged; actual cadence can be limited by the menu/audio service rate.
- Release, focus loss, lobby row/screen changes, overlay entry, pause entry and resume countdown reset repeats. Delayed service ticks apply at most one step per held control, avoiding catch-up bursts.
- Gauge/random selection, navigation and text deletion retain their cadence. Mouse-button holds are outside this change. ×0.5/×2 remain once per press.
- Judgement, score and replay formats are unchanged. Latency refers to the existing visual offset, not the input judgement offset.
