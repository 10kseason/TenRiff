# Constellations in the song selection background

[한국어](menu-constellations.ko.md) | [English](menu-constellations.en.md) | [日本語](menu-constellations.ja.md) | [简体中文](menu-constellations.zh-CN.md)

Three small decorative constellations inspired by Aries, Libra and Cancer appear in the dark interior of the built-in song selection video. Fine lines, cool halos and sparse surrounding stars suggest a telescope view. This is not an astronomical observation chart.

The existing renderer draws them above the video and below menu text and panels, without re-encoding the video or loading additional images. Positions remain fixed; brightness varies slowly and slightly. System or application reduced motion freezes the effect.

Custom skin backgrounds/videos, title, results and gameplay are unaffected. The original video, judgements and records are unchanged. The supplied reference image stays in local working material and is not a runtime asset.
