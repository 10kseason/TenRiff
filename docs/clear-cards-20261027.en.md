# Song-select clear cards

- EZ CLEAR is green, NM CLEAR yellow, HD CLEAR red and EX CLEAR a static rainbow gradient. The frame, badge and built-in vector mark share the clear color.

- Explicit skin jacket/status rectangles and imported mark images take precedence. New placement changes the default layout; imported logo pixels are retained.

- Stored CLEAR/GAUGE SHIFT/gauge records are unchanged. Assisted, failed and unplayed songs do not receive an official clear badge. Scoring, judgement, replays and gameplay gauge colors remain unchanged.
